Welcome to When Coffee Meets Whisky, Baltics' first listening bar, where bold flavors and deep sounds create an unforgettable experience.

Our story began a decade ago with T Studio, the music shop next door, fueling our passion and setting the rhythm of our nights.

In a space with elevated atmosphere and refined acoustics, specialty coffee meets the finest Japanese whisky selection. Interior by Binski Studio, sound by Reflector Audio: a place where the art of sound isn't just enjoyed, it's celebrated.
